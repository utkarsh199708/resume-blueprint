Data Analyst, Growth

Brightcart is a grocery delivery app used by 1.2 million households. Our Growth team decides where we spend our marketing budget and which features make customers come back. We are hiring a Data Analyst to be the team's source of truth.

In this role you will:
* Build and maintain dashboards in Looker that track acquisition, activation and retention
* Analyze A/B tests with product managers and recommend what to ship
* Write SQL against our Snowflake warehouse to answer ad hoc questions from marketing
* Partner with data engineering on data quality and new tracking events
* Present findings to the leadership team every month
* Identify the drivers of churn and size the opportunity in revenue terms

You have:
* 2+ years of experience in an analytics role
* Advanced SQL; you can write window functions without looking them up
* Experience with Python (pandas) or R for analysis
* A solid grasp of statistics and experimentation
* The ability to turn messy data into a clear story for non-technical stakeholders

It's a plus if you have:
* Experience with dbt
* Worked in e-commerce or a consumer app
* Familiarity with Amplitude or Mixpanel

Hybrid: 3 days a week in our London office.
